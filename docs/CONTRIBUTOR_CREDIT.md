# Contributor and tool provenance policy

The repository may be developed with human planning, Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT or other tools. Public documentation can prioritize functionality over the identity of the software used to write code; however:

- Preserve actual Git authorship, maintainers' and outside contributors' rights and historical records.
- Never forge commit names, timestamps, authored PRs or human review evidence.
- Do not remove third-party credits or licenses.
- Follow upstream maintainers' contribution policies when sending patches to their repositories.
- Accurately disclose AI assistance if specifically requested by maintainers, reviewers, funders or application forms, or legally required.
- Avoid representing automated contributions as human-written to manipulate public metrics or eligibility.

Assessment standard: reproducibility, safety, maintenance and real usefulness, regardless of tooling. Attribution is about accuracy rather than marketing slogans.
