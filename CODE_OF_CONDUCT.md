# Community code of conduct

We want a constructive space for game developers, technical artists and contributors of different experience levels.

- Treat people respectfully; critique implementations and evidence, not identities.
- No harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing or malicious disclosure of private projects.
- No spam, engagement farming, fabricated benchmarks or coordinated metric manipulation.
- Give accurate credit for others' work and follow upstream licenses.
- Security issues must be reported privately, not used to embarrass contributors.

Moderators may edit/remove inappropriate content or restrict participation when necessary. To report a concern, contact maintainers through a private GitHub channel established at public launch; until then, do not publish sensitive reports to issues.
